## Important distinctions

The strongest matches for mutual restrictions on soliciting the other contracting parties’ employees are the Leclanché, Coherus BioSciences, and Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory agreements.

The FTE Networks, Quaker Chemical, Coral Gold Resources, and Kubient agreements also satisfy the question, but their restrictions are primarily unilateral.

The Theravance agreement is included because its executive is a contracting party who agrees not to solicit the employer’s employees. It is an employment-related agreement rather than a business-to-business non-solicitation covenant.
