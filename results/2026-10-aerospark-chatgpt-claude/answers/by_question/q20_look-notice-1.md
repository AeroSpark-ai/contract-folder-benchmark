I'll look for the agreement in your "Benchmark Test" shared drive.

**At least 30 days' notice.** Under Section 8.1 (Automatic Renewal), the agreement automatically renews year to year at the end of the Initial Term or any Renewal Term. It renews unless either party notifies the other, at least 30 days before the current term ends, that it does not intend to renew.

A few details matter for timing:

- **Term dates:** The Initial Term runs from the Effective Date, September 30, 1999, through the day before its fourth anniversary, which is September 29, 2003. After that, each renewal runs one year. So non-renewal notice has to arrive at least 30 days before the end of whichever term is running at the time.
- **Form of notice:** Section 13.5 requires notices to be in writing. They can be delivered in person against a signed receipt, sent by nationally recognized overnight courier, or sent by fax. A notice counts from when it is received, not when it is sent. A fax sent after 3:00 p.m. Philadelphia time counts from the next business day.

The agreement can also be ended early for an uncured material breach under Section 8.2, but that is separate from stopping renewal.

This means either party must notify the other at least 30 days before the current term expires. Otherwise, the agreement automatically renews for another year.
