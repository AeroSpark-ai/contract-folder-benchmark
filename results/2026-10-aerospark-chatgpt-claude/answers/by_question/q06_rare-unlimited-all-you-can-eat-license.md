## Unlimited-usage licenses found

I’ve confirmed an explicit unlimited license in the Lifeway Foods Endorsement Agreement (March 16, 2016).

The agreement grants Lifeway an “unlimited, perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide” license to use the individual's name, image, likeness, and related endorsement materials. The rights include reproduction, publication, broadcasting, display, and creation of derivative works. The grant is subject to the agreement's terms, including a royalty exception referenced in Section 9.

[Open Lifeway Foods contract](https://drive.google.com/file/d/REDACTED/view?usp=drivesdk)

This is an unlimited grant within a specified category of rights, not blanket permission to use any intellectual property for any purpose.

Two additional contracts clearly qualify:

1\. [Entrust Inc. – Strategic Alliance Agreement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/REDACTED/view?usp=drivesdk) (July 24, 1998). Section 3.01 grants Nortel and its affiliates a “non-exclusive, fully paid-up, worldwide, perpetual license to use an unlimited number of copies of the Entrust Products.” The grant is subject to the terms of a separate Enterprise License to be concluded between the parties.

2\. [Corio Inc. – License and Hosting Agreement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/REDACTED/view?usp=drivesdk) (July 20, 2000). Section 2.1 permits sublicensing to an unlimited number of customers; Section 2.6 provides unlimited access to the MarketSite.net service; and Exhibit B lists unlimited software users. Usage is restricted to authorized hosting arrangements and subject to applicable fees, revenue-sharing provisions, and other contract conditions.

### Additional qualified match

[Iovance Biotherapeutics – Strategic Alliance Agreement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/REDACTED/view?usp=drivesdk) (August 3, 2017). Section 7.3(b) grants a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual data license with sublicensing rights and expressly provides “unrestricted rights” to use specified melanoma-study data in governmental and regulatory submissions. This is unrestricted use for a defined purpose, rather than a general unlimited-usage license. Section 7.6 also establishes conditions for the license provisions to become effective.
