## Important qualifications

The findings distinguish employee-related restrictions from customer non-solicitation clauses, and first-refusal/offer/negotiation rights from ordinary exclusivity and general commercial negotiation language.

These are historical contract documents, and this review does not establish whether they remain in force, whether rights have expired or been exercised, or whether particular employee restrictions are legally enforceable.

Most important for a combined obligations review: the Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory agreement contains both employee non-solicitation restrictions and first-offer/first-refusal rights. The PHL agreement merits a separate drafting review before relying on its personnel restriction.
