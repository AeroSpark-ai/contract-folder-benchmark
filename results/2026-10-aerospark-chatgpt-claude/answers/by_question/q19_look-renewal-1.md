I'll look for that agreement in your "Benchmark Test" shared drive.

The renewal term is one year. The agreement doesn't spell out how renewal works beyond that.

**Maintenance and support (Section 10):** Garman provides its Maintenance Services "for a renewable one-year term" starting on the Maintenance Commencement Date. That date is when acceptance testing has been successfully completed at all Authorized Locations. Renewal depends on Sparkling paying the maintenance fees in the Fee Schedule. The services are software updates, telephone support and software repair. The agreement gives no automatic-renewal mechanism, notice period or renewal procedure.

**The agreement and license overall (Section 17(a)):** these have no fixed term. They stay in effect perpetually as long as Sparkling pays the fees in the Fee Schedule and the agreement isn't terminated. Sparkling can terminate at any time on 90 calendar days' written notice. Either party can terminate for insolvency-type events, or for a material default not cured within 30 days of notice.

**Related warranty (Section 12):** the performance warranty runs for the acceptance period plus one year from the Maintenance Commencement Date. After that it continues for as long as the Software is covered by Maintenance Services.
